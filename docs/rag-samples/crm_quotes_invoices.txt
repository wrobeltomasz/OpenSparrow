CRM Quotes and Invoices
=========================

NOTE ON VISIBILITY:
Neither Quotes nor Invoices is in the sidebar menu in this demo. Both are seeded and
fully configured, and the normal way to reach them is the Quotes and Invoices subtables
on a deal's edit page (or a direct index.php?table=quotes / ?table=invoices URL).

QUOTES
------
A Quote is a priced offer sent to a client for a specific Deal.

Fields:
- quote_number: Unique identifier (e.g. Q-2026-001)
- deal_id: Deal this quote belongs to
- status: Draft, Sent, Accepted, Rejected, Expired
- valid_until: Offer expiry date
- subtotal: Sum before tax
- tax: Tax amount (e.g. 23% VAT)
- total: Grand total
- notes: Internal or client-facing comments
- created_at: Set automatically, read-only

Quote lifecycle:
1. Draft — being prepared
2. Sent — delivered to the client
3. Accepted — client agreed
4. Rejected — client declined
5. Expired — valid_until date passed without a response

AUTOMATION — "Quote Accepted — Generate Draft Invoice":
As soon as a quote is updated to status = Accepted, a Draft invoice is created
automatically. It links back to the same deal and quote, copies the quote's net/tax/total
amounts, sets invoice_number to INV-AUTO-Q{quote id}, and sets due_date to the quote's
valid_until date. The invoice only needs reviewing and moving from Draft to Sent.
The automation also appends an "[Invoiced]" marker to the quote's notes, and its own
condition excludes quotes whose notes already contain that marker — so it fires at most
once per quote, even if the quote is edited again while Accepted.

Quotes are also the final step of the Convert Lead workflow ("Send Quote"), which allows
multiple quotes per deal.

INVOICES
--------
An Invoice is a payment request. It can be generated from a Quote by the automation above
or created directly against a Deal.

Fields:
- invoice_number: Unique identifier (e.g. INV-2026-005)
- deal_id: Deal this invoice belongs to
- quote_id: Source quote, if the invoice came from one
- status: Draft, Sent, Paid, Overdue, Cancelled
- issue_date: Date the invoice was issued (required)
- due_date: Payment deadline (required)
- amount_net: Net amount before tax
- amount_tax: Tax charged
- amount_total: Total payable
- paid_at: Timestamp of payment (NULL if unpaid)
- notes: Internal notes
- created_at: Set automatically, read-only

Invoice statuses:
- Draft: not yet sent
- Sent: delivered, awaiting payment
- Paid: payment received (paid_at is set)
- Overdue: due_date passed without payment
- Cancelled: voided

HIDDEN VIEWS:
Two revenue views are installed but hidden from the menu:
- Revenue Summary: invoice count and total by status. Its total column uses a SUMIF so
  Draft invoices are excluded from the summed amount.
- Revenue by Period: invoices grouped by month, with subtotals, a SUMIF for paid revenue
  and a COUNTIF for unpaid invoices.
