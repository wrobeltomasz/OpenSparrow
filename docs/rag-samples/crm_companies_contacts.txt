CRM Companies and Contacts
==========================
The two base tables of the CRM demo. Companies is the top-level menu item; Contacts is
nested underneath it in the sidebar.

COMPANIES
---------
A Company is a client organization.

Fields:
- name: Official company name (required)
- industry: Sector the company operates in (e.g. Technology, Consulting, Cybersecurity)
- website: Company website URL
- phone: Main company phone number
- email: Company email address
- created_at: Set automatically, read-only, hidden from the grid and the edit form

Related records on a company's edit page:
- Contacts subtable: first name, last name, email, position
- Deals subtable: title, stage, value, expected close

Industries used by the seed data include Technology, Consulting, Software, IT Services,
Digital Agency, Cloud Services, Analytics, Cybersecurity, R&D, Management Consulting,
Business Services, Enterprise Software, Private Equity, Marketing Services and
Venture Capital.

CONTACTS
--------
A Contact is a person working at a Company.

Fields:
- company_id: Company this contact belongs to (dropdown showing the company name)
- first_name, last_name: Contact name (both required)
- email: Contact email address
- phone: Contact phone number
- position: Job title (e.g. Sales Director, IT Director, Head of Sales)
- created_at: Set automatically, read-only

Photos:
Contacts have an image gallery enabled, labelled "Photo", up to 5 images per record, and
the first image is shown in the grid. The demo ships no photos of its own — the gallery
starts empty and you upload your own.

Related records on a contact's edit page:
- Activities subtable: type, scheduled at, done

Where else contacts appear:
- As the primary contact on a Deal (deals.contact_id)
- As extra stakeholders on a Deal via the "Other Stakeholders" many-to-many field
- As the assignee on an Asset (assets.assigned_contact_id)
- As an interested party on a Product (product_contacts many-to-many)

CREATING RECORDS:
- Directly from the Companies or Contacts grid ("Add" button)
- Through the "New CRM Deal" workflow, which chains company → contact → deal → activity
- Through the "Add Contact" workflow, which validates the email and phone with a
  PostgreSQL stored procedure before saving

DASHBOARD:
- "Companies" stat card counts all company records
- "Contacts" stat card counts all contact records

FILES:
Companies and Contacts are both file-relation targets, so documents can be attached to
them and browsed from Menu → Files.
