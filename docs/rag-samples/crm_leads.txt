CRM Leads — Prospect Management
=================================
A Lead is an incoming prospect who has not yet been converted into a Contact.

NOTE ON VISIBILITY:
Leads are NOT in the sidebar menu in this demo. The table is seeded and fully configured,
but you reach it through the Convert Lead workflow or a direct index.php?table=leads URL.
It is kept as the working surface for two automations and the GDPR anonymization rules.

FIELDS:
- source: How the lead was acquired (Web, Referral, Cold Call, Event, Ads, Other)
- first_name, last_name: Lead's name (both required)
- email, phone: Contact details
- company_name: Free text company (not yet linked to the companies table)
- status: Qualification stage
- converted_contact_id: Filled when the lead is converted to a Contact
- created_at: Set automatically, read-only

LEAD STATUSES (with their grid colors):
1. New — Just captured, no action taken (blue)
2. Contacted — Initial outreach made (yellow)
3. Qualified — Need and budget confirmed, ready for conversion (green)
4. Lost — Did not proceed (red)

LEAD SOURCES:
- Web: form submission or inbound inquiry
- Referral: introduced by an existing client or partner
- Cold Call: outbound sales call
- Event: trade show, conference, or webinar
- Ads: paid campaign (PPC, social)
- Other: anything not classified above

CONVERSION WORKFLOW (Convert Lead):
Step 1: Capture Lead — fill the lead record with source and contact info
Step 2: Add Company — create a company record for their organization
Step 3: Add Contact — create a contact linked to that company
Step 4: Create Deal — open a deal for the opportunity

AUTOMATIONS ON LEADS:
- "New Web Lead — Auto Contact": any new lead created with source = Web is automatically
  set to status = Contacted, since a web form submission already counts as first contact.
- "New Lead — Welcome Email": any new lead that has an email address is sent a
  "Thank you for your interest" welcome email. The message is queued into
  spw_automation_emails and delivered by cron_notifications.php, so it needs working SMTP
  settings and the cron job running.

DATA RETENTION (GDPR ANONYMIZATION):
Stale leads are the demo's anonymization target: any lead older than 365 days by
created_at has its email, phone, first name and last name replaced with placeholders
(anonymized@example.com and [REDACTED]). Frequency is set to "manual", so nothing runs
unattended — go to Admin → Anonymization and use Preview (dry run) to see which leads
would be affected before running it. The seed deliberately includes leads older than a
year so the preview is not empty.

LEADS FUNNEL VIEW:
A "Leads Funnel" view (lead count per status) is installed but hidden from the menu.
