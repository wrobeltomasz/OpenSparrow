CRM Workflows
=============
A workflow is a guided, multi-step wizard that creates a chain of linked records. Each
step targets a table; a step's foreign key is filled automatically from the record created
in an earlier step. The CRM demo installs three.

1. NEW CRM DEAL
---------------
The standard "land a new client" path.
Step 1: Add Company    → companies
Step 2: Add Contact    → contacts, company_id taken from step 1, repeatable
Step 3: Create Deal    → deals, company_id taken from step 1
Step 4: Log Activity   → activities, deal_id taken from step 3, repeatable

"Repeatable" (allow multiple) means you can add several records at that step before
moving on — several contacts at one company, several activities on one deal.

2. CONVERT LEAD
---------------
Turns an inbound prospect into a real opportunity.
Step 1: Capture Lead   → leads
Step 2: Add Company    → companies
Step 3: Add Contact    → contacts, company_id taken from step 2
Step 4: Create Deal    → deals, company_id taken from step 2

This workflow is the main way to reach the leads table, since it is not in the sidebar
menu.

3. ADD CONTACT (with stored-procedure validation)
-------------------------------------------------
A single-step workflow that exists to demonstrate the per-step stored-procedure hook.
Step 1: Add Contact    → contacts

Before the step is committed, the workflow CALLs the PostgreSQL procedure
spw_crm.validate_contact(email, phone) with the values typed into the form. If the
procedure raises an exception the step is blocked and the message is shown in the UI —
nothing is written to the database.

What the procedure rejects:
- An email that is NULL or contains no "@" → "Invalid email: ..."
- A phone that, after stripping separators, is not 6 to 15 digits with an optional
  leading "+" → "Invalid phone: ... digits only, separators - . ( ) and spaces are
  allowed"

Separators are stripped before the digit check, so "+1-555-1001" and "+1 (555) 1001" both
pass. An empty phone is accepted; only the email is mandatory.

REQUIRED FIELDS:
Workflow steps enforce the same required fields as the normal edit form — for example
companies.name, contacts.first_name / last_name and deals.title are all NOT NULL and must
be filled before the step advances.
