CRM Dashboard and Calendar
==========================

DASHBOARD
---------
The CRM demo installs seven widgets. Nothing else is on the dashboard unless you add it.

Stat cards (one row):
- "Companies" — count of all company records
- "Contacts" — count of all contact records
- "Pipeline Value" — sum of deals.value across deals whose stage is neither Won nor Lost,
  i.e. the value still in play

Charts:
- "Deals by Stage" — bar chart, deals grouped by stage
- "Activities Status" — pie chart, activities grouped by done (completed vs pending)
- "Deals Value Over Time" — area/line chart, deal value summed per month by created_at
- "Activities Over Time" — area/line chart, activity count per week by created_at

PERIOD FILTER:
The dashboard has a period filter (Today / 7 days / 30 days) and stat cards show a trend
delta against the previous period. The demo seed deliberately spreads created_at over the
past months for companies, contacts, deals, products and activities so the filter and the
time-series charts have real history to show. Leads older than a year are left untouched
so the anonymization preview still has stale rows to match.

There are no widgets for leads, quotes, invoices, products or assets — those tables are
not part of the demo's visible surface.

CALENDAR
--------
Two sources feed the Calendar:

1. Activities, by scheduled_at
   - Color: blue
   - Title: the activity type; subtitle: the notes
   - Notification: 1 day before scheduled_at
   - Click opens edit.php?table=activities&id={id}

2. Deals, by expected_close
   - Color: yellow
   - Title: the deal title
   - Notification: 3 days before expected_close
   - Click opens edit.php?table=deals&id={id}

Quotes, invoices and asset warranty dates are NOT on the calendar in this demo.

Calendar notifications are delivered by cron_notifications.php, which also handles note
reminders and queued automation emails. Recipients are configured per source in
Admin → Calendar; the demo leaves the recipient list empty, so set it there if you want
the reminders to actually reach a user.
