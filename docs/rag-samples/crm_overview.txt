CRM Demo — OpenSparrow
======================
Customer Relationship Management demo app for tracking companies, contacts, deals and
sales activities. Installed from Admin → Demo Data. All tables live in the PostgreSQL
schema spw_crm.

MENU (what the demo puts in the sidebar):
- Dashboard
- Calendar
- Companies (with Contacts nested underneath)
- Deals
- Activities
- Deals Board (Kanban)
- Files
Plus the Pipeline Summary view, and the Pipeline Report and Activity Agenda printouts.

TABLES IN THE MENU:
- companies: Client organizations. Fields: name, industry, website, phone, email.
- contacts: People at companies. Fields: first_name, last_name, email, phone, position,
  company_id. Contacts also have a photo gallery (up to 5 images per record).
- deals: Sales opportunities. Fields: title, value, stage, expected_close, company_id,
  contact_id.
- activities: Actions on deals (calls, meetings, emails). Fields: type, notes,
  scheduled_at, done, deal_id, contact_id.

BACKGROUND TABLES (seeded and fully configured, but NOT in the menu):
Reachable only through a deal's subtables, a workflow step, or a direct
index.php?table=... URL. They exist so the demo can show automations, a workflow and
the GDPR anonymization rules working against real data.
- leads: Incoming prospects not yet converted. Drives two automations and the
  anonymization rules.
- quotes: Pricing offers per deal. Drives the "Quote Accepted" invoice automation and is
  the last step of the Convert Lead workflow. Shown as a subtable on a deal.
- invoices: Payment requests. Shown as a subtable on a deal.
- products / product_contacts: Product catalog and a many-to-many link to interested
  contacts.
- assets: Company-owned asset registry.
- deal_contacts: Junction table behind the "Other Stakeholders" many-to-many field on a
  deal.

RELATIONSHIPS:
- Contact belongs to Company (company_id)
- Deal belongs to Company and Contact (contact_id = primary contact)
- Deal has many extra stakeholder Contacts via deal_contacts (many-to-many field
  "Other Stakeholders")
- Activity belongs to Deal and Contact
- Quote belongs to Deal
- Invoice belongs to Deal and optionally a Quote
- Asset can be assigned to a Contact (assigned_contact_id)
- Product to Contact is many-to-many via product_contacts

SUBTABLES (related records listed on an edit page):
- Company → Contacts, Deals
- Contact → Activities
- Deal → Activities, Quotes, Invoices

WORKFLOWS:
1. New CRM Deal: Add Company → Add Contact → Create Deal → Log Activity
2. Convert Lead: Capture Lead → Add Company → Add Contact → Create Deal → Send Quote
3. Add Contact: single step, validated by a PostgreSQL stored procedure
See the CRM Workflows doc.

VIEWS:
- Pipeline Summary (in the menu): deal and activity measures per company and stage
Four more views are installed but hidden from the menu: Leads Funnel, Revenue Summary,
Assets Summary, Revenue by Period. Two further views (Pipeline Report Source, Activity
Agenda Source) exist only to feed the print templates and are not meant to be browsed.

DEMO USERS:
Three extra users are created: demo.anna (editor), demo.marek (editor) and demo.julia
(viewer). Password for all three is "test". They own records, write comments and receive
notifications — see the CRM Collaboration doc.

UNINSTALL:
Admin → Demo Data removes the spw_crm schema, the demo users and every config entry the
install added (widgets, views, menu items, board, workflows, automations, prints,
anonymization rules, files).
