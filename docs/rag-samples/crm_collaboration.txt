CRM Collaboration — Users, Comments, Notes, Files, Notifications
================================================================
The demo seeds a small team and the traces of their work, so the collaboration features
are not empty on a fresh install.

DEMO USERS
----------
Three users are created alongside the CRM data:
- demo.anna  — role: editor
- demo.marek — role: editor
- demo.julia — role: viewer
The password for all three is "test". Each gets an avatar colour; the avatar itself is the
first letter of the username.

Roles behave as they do everywhere in OpenSparrow: an editor can create and change
records, a viewer is read-only.

COMMENTS
--------
Records carry threaded comments, shown on the record's edit page. The demo seeds a set of
comments forming real back-and-forth discussions between the three users, mostly on deals,
plus some on companies, contacts and leads. Examples of the threads: pricing and
discount tiers on "Enterprise License Q2", stalled legal redlines on the digital
transformation deal, a reference-customer request on the analytics deal, and handover
notes on a deal that was closed Won.

MY NOTES
--------
"My Notes" is a private per-user panel — a note is visible only to its author. A note can
stand alone or be linked to a record (a deal, a company), and can carry a reminder_date.

The demo seeds notes across all three users, some linked to a deal or a company and some
standalone. A couple of them carry a reminder date (one due today, one tomorrow) so the
note-reminder flow in cron_notifications.php has something to fire on.

MY RECORDS (ownership)
----------------------
Records can be assigned an owner, and each user sees the records they own in the "My
records" panel. The demo hands out a handful of records so the panel is not empty for any
of the three: demo.anna owns deals and a contact, demo.marek owns a deal and Acme
Corporation, demo.julia owns a deal.

The panel's label for a record comes from a per-table column mapping — companies show
their name, contacts show first and last name, deals show the title, activities show the
type, and so on.

Ownership is enforced on reads and on bulk operations, not just displayed: a record whose
current owner is someone else is filtered out. Records with no owner stay visible to
everyone, so assigning an owner narrows access rather than the reverse.

FILES
-----
Menu → Files lists documents attached to records. File relations are configured for
Companies, Deals and Contacts, so those three can hold attachments.

The demo seeds a few small CSV attachments: a pricing breakdown and a scope list on deals,
a quarterly account summary on Acme Corporation, and a call log on the contact John Smith.
They are written both to spw_files and to disk under storage/files/.

Contacts additionally have a record image gallery (label "Photo", up to 5 per record). The
demo ships no images — it used to seed contact photographs, which were dropped for
licensing and likeness reasons, so the gallery starts empty.

NOTIFICATIONS
-------------
The bell icon lists in-app notifications. The demo seeds a batch of them across the three
users — new comments on deals, companies, contacts and leads, plus ownership assignments —
with a mix of read and unread so the panel looks lived-in.

Notifications are also produced at runtime by:
- the "Deal Won — Close Notification" automation
- calendar reminders (activities 1 day ahead, deals 3 days ahead)
- note reminders, when a note's reminder_date comes due
The runtime ones are delivered by cron_notifications.php, so that job has to be running.
